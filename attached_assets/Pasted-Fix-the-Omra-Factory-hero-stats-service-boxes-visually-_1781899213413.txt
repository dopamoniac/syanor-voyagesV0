Fix the Omra Factory hero stats/service boxes visually on both mobile and desktop.

Current problem:
The four stats boxes under the hero are not polished enough. On desktop they look too flat, misaligned, and partially cramped inside the hero area. On mobile they look basic, too boxed, with weak spacing and the layout does not feel premium. The boxes should look like luxury website UI, not simple AI-generated blocks.

Section to fix:
Stats/service strip with these four items:

1. Depuis 2026
   Saisons Omra

2. 6 villes
   De départ

3. Standard à VIP
   Niveaux de confort

4. Hôtels sélectionnés
   Proche des lieux saints

Design goal:
Make this stats strip feel premium, elegant, clean, and fully responsive. It should match the white ivory Omra Factory luxury theme with emerald and gold accents.

Desktop requirements:

* Create one beautiful horizontal stats panel under the hero text.
* The panel should be centered and aligned with the hero content.
* Use a soft ivory / pearl / champagne background.
* Add subtle glass effect or soft luxury card background.
* Add light gold borders or dividers between items.
* Add gentle shadow, not heavy.
* Use rounded corners, around 24px to 32px.
* Keep all four stats equally spaced.
* Icons should be gold and aligned nicely with the text.
* Text should not be squeezed.
* Nothing should overflow or be cut.
* The panel should not cover the Kaaba badly.
* Give enough margin above and below.
* Make it look like a premium booking/travel feature strip.

Mobile requirements:

* Convert the stats into a clean 2x2 grid.
* Each stat should be its own mini-card.
* Cards should have rounded corners, soft ivory background, subtle gold border, and light shadow.
* Keep good padding inside each card.
* Icons should sit above or beside the text in a balanced way.
* Font sizes should be readable but not oversized.
* The cards should not feel cramped.
* The grid should have consistent gaps.
* Make sure the section does not overlap the hero image or buttons.
* On very small screens, allow the cards to stack into one column if needed.
* Keep the premium white theme, not black.

Visual style:

* Background: #FFFDF8, #F8F2E8, #F3E8D5
* Gold accent: #B88A44
* Emerald text: #063B32
* Muted text: #66645F
* Borders: rgba(184, 138, 68, 0.22)
* Shadows: soft and warm, not dark

Suggested layout:
Desktop:
Use CSS grid with 4 columns:
grid-template-columns: repeat(4, minmax(0, 1fr));

Tablet:
Use 2 columns:
grid-template-columns: repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr));

Mobile:
Use 2 columns by default, then 1 column under 380px if necessary.
