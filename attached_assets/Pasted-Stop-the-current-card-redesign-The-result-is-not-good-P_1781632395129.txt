Stop the current card redesign. The result is not good.

Problem:
- Mobile cards are too dark and heavy.
- Images are barely visible.
- Cards feel too big, uncomfortable, and not pleasant to scan.
- The layout looks forced and not premium.
- Text is taking over the image.
- The cards do not feel clean or luxury.

I want a cleaner, brighter, more balanced card system for BOTH desktop and mobile.

Main direction:
Use premium image-first cards, but make the image clearly visible and beautifully cropped.

Mobile requirements:
- Each card should be vertical, clean, and easy to read.
- Image must be fully visible at the top of the card.
- Image height: around 170px to 210px.
- Use object-fit: cover.
- Use object-position: center.
- Do NOT cover the whole image with dark overlay.
- Only add a soft bottom gradient if text is on the image.
- Better option: put text BELOW the image on a clean ivory or emerald content panel.
- Cards should not be too tall.
- Cards should have balanced padding: 18px to 22px.
- Border radius: 24px.
- Gap between cards: 22px.
- CTA must be clear and aligned at the bottom.
- No cramped text.
- No huge empty dark blocks.

Desktop requirements:
- Use a clean responsive grid.
- Image should be visible and attractive.
- Cards should feel editorial and premium.
- Do not make all cards huge dark rectangles.
- Keep spacing airy and elegant.

Recommended card structure:
Card:
1. Top image area
   - height 190px mobile
   - height 240px desktop
   - rounded top corners
   - badge top-left
2. Bottom content area
   - ivory or deep emerald depending on category
   - title
   - short description
   - small meta row
   - CTA button or arrow
